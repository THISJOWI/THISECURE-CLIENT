THISECURE PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: July 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how THISECURE ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data when you use our mobile application, web services, and related services (the "Service").

This Policy is drafted in compliance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation or "GDPR") and the Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 on Data Protection and Digital Rights Guarantee (LOPDGDD).

If you are using a self-hosted instance of THISECURE, the data controller is the organization or individual operating that instance, not THISECURE. Please review their privacy policy for details on how they handle your data.

2. PERSONAL DATA WE COLLECT

2.1. Data you provide directly:

- Email address
- Full name
- Country of residence
- Birth date (optional)
- Phone number (optional)
- Biography (optional)
- Profile avatar image (optional)
- Account type (Community or Business)
- Hosting mode preference (Cloud or Self-hosted)

2.2. Data generated through use of the Service:

- Authentication credentials (BCrypt-hashed password, JWT tokens, OAuth2 tokens)
- Encrypted password vault entries (titles, usernames, passwords, website URLs)
- Encrypted notes (titles and content)
- OTP/TOTP authenticator configurations (seeds, issuers, labels)
- Encrypted messages (content, sender/receiver identifiers, timestamps)
- End-to-end encryption keys (X25519 public key)
- Login activity (timestamps, failed attempts, lockout events)
- Sync events (identifiers only, not content)
- Subscription and payment data (Stripe customer ID, subscription status)
- UI preferences (theme, language, notification settings)

2.3. Data collected through third-party authentication:

When you use Google, GitHub, or Microsoft to sign in, we receive:
- Email address
- Name
- Profile picture URL
- Provider-specific identifier

2.4. Data collected via LDAP (Business accounts only):

When your organization uses LDAP authentication, we may receive:
- Email address
- Full name
- Profile photo (jpegPhoto)
- Phone number
- Country
- Department or organizational unit
- Directory attributes configured by your organization

2.5. Telemetry and analytics data:

When using the cloud-hosted Service with telemetry enabled, we collect anonymous usage data via Aptabase:
- Screen views (page names only, no content)
- App version and platform (iOS, Android, desktop)
- Session timestamps
- Authentication events: login, login failed, registration, registration failed, logout, social login (Google, GitHub, Microsoft)
- Password management events: password created, password deleted
- Note management events: note created, note deleted
- OTP management events: OTP created, OTP deleted
- Account events: account deleted, password changed, biometric toggled, data imported, data exported
- Navigation and deployment events: environment switched, self-hosted profile added, search performed
- Registration events: self-hosted account registered
- No personal identifiers are associated with telemetry data

Telemetry is disabled by default in self-hosted instances and can be opted out of at any time from the application settings.

2.6. Location data:

When you use the country selection map feature, your selected geographic coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap (Nominatim API) for reverse geocoding. THISECURE does not store precise location coordinates — only the selected country name is stored in your profile.

4. DATA STORAGE AND ENCRYPTION

4.1. End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)

Messages exchanged between users are protected by end-to-end encryption using the X25519 key exchange protocol combined with HKDF key derivation and AES-256-GCM symmetric encryption. We cannot access, read, or decrypt the content of your messages.

4.2. Server-Side Encryption

Password vault entries, notes, and OTP secrets are encrypted at rest on our servers using AES-256-GCM with 256-bit keys. LDAP bind passwords are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The encryption keys are separate from authentication keys and are never shared with third parties.

4.3. Data at Rest

Your data is stored across multiple secure databases:
- PostgreSQL databases for user accounts, profiles, subscription data, passwords, notes, OTP configurations, and organizations
- Apache Cassandra for messages
- MongoDB for messaging service data (conversations, LDAP user cache)
- Redis/KeyDB for temporary session data, OTP verification codes, and revoked tokens
- Cloudflare R2 (S3-compatible) for avatar image storage (cloud-hosted only)
- Local filesystem for avatar storage (self-hosted only)

4.4. Storage Locations

Our cloud infrastructure is hosted in the European Union. Cloudflare R2 object storage (used for avatars) may process data in the United States under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

7. DATA PROCESSING UNDER LDAP

When your organization integrates THISECURE with Active Directory or LDAP:
- Authentication is performed against your organization's LDAP server
- Directory attributes (name, email, photo, phone, department) are synchronized to provide the Service
- THISECURE processes this data on behalf of your organization as a data processor
- Your organization remains the data controller for LDAP-sourced data
- LDAP bind credentials are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM)

10. YOUR RIGHTS

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

10.1. Right of Access (Art. 15)
You can request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.

10.2. Right to Rectification (Art. 16)
You can correct inaccurate or incomplete data through your profile settings.

10.3. Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten") (Art. 17)
You can delete your account, which will permanently remove your personal data from our systems. Account deletion is irreversible — data cannot be recovered after deletion.

10.4. Right to Restrict Processing (Art. 18)
You can request that we limit how we use your data.

10.5. Right to Data Portability (Art. 20)
You can export your data through the application's import/export feature in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, or request a manual export.

10.6. Right to Object (Art. 21)
You can object to processing based on legitimate interest, including telemetry collection.

10.7. Right to Withdraw Consent
Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@thisjowi.uk. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are unsatisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) at www.aepd.es.

11. DATA SECURITY

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:
- End-to-end encryption (X25519 + HKDF + AES-256-GCM)
- Server-side encryption at rest (AES-256-GCM)
- BCrypt password hashing (cost factor 12)
- JWT token authentication with HMAC-SHA256 signing
- TLS/HTTPS for all data in transit
- Rate limiting on authentication endpoints
- Account lockout after failed login attempts (progressive: 5min, 15min, 1hr)
- Biometric authentication option (device-level)
- Input validation and HTML escaping on user-provided content
- File type and size validation on uploads
- HMAC-signed inter-service communication via Kafka
- Regular security scanning (CodeQL, Trivy, Gitleaks) in CI pipeline
- HashiCorp Vault for dynamic database credentials (cloud-hosted)

12. MINORS

The Service is not intended for individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you become aware that a minor has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.
